# Final split: P1 + val-tiles by spatial block (split_v1)

Generated by `scripts/make_split.py`; the split itself is `splits/split_v1.csv` (3,732 tiles; columns `disaster`, `tile_id`, `part`). The block each tile belongs to is in `splits/split_v1_blocks.csv`. This replaces the tile-by-tile val-tiles of the "P1 + val-tiles" candidate in `docs/split_candidates.md`, so the numbers here differ from the ones there.

- **train**: guatemala-volcano, hurricane-harvey, hurricane-matthew, mexico-earthquake, palu-tsunami, socal-fire
- **val-tiles**: whole spatial blocks of the train disasters, about 10% of each disaster's tiles (seed 0)
- **val**: hurricane-florence, midwest-flooding
- **test**: hurricane-michael, santa-rosa-wildfire

## Blocks

**Block size: 0.02 degrees** of longitude and latitude (about 2.2 km north-south; a tile is about 0.005 degrees wide and 0.0045 tall, so a block is roughly 4 tiles across). Each tile's position comes from fitting its buildings' pixel coordinates to their lng/lat coordinates; a block is one cell of a fixed lng/lat grid. Per disaster the blocks were shuffled and walked once, holding out a block whenever that brought the held-out tile count closer to 10%.

Two things the grid alone does not handle:

- **Tiles without buildings have no coordinates** (615 tiles in the train disasters, most of them socal-fire). They cannot be placed in a block, so they all stay in train, and the 10% target counts only tiles with buildings: val-tiles is 10.3% of the tiles with buildings and 7.4% of all tiles in the train disasters. Empty tiles contribute no building crops to either side.
- **Some tile footprints overlap** (112 pairs in the train disasters share more than 1% of a tile's area). Grid cells holding such a pair were merged into one block, so no overlapping pair is split between train and val-tiles (last column below). A one-off check of the pre-disaster images found that overlapping tiles do not show the same picture in the shared area, which agrees with check 2 in `docs/split_candidates.md` (no building appears in two tiles): they behave like neighbours, not duplicates.

## Remaining leak

Two tiles *touch* when the gap between their footprints is under 5% of a tile, corner-to-corner included. A val-tiles block *touches a train block* when at least one of its tiles touches a train tile.

**26 of 38 val-tiles blocks sit next to a train block** (76 of 163 val-tiles tiles touch a train tile). Blocking keeps a held-out tile's neighbours inside its own block where it can, but a block's outer edge still borders train, so val-tiles remains a same-disaster score and will read higher than val. Empty train tiles are not in this count because their position is unknown.
