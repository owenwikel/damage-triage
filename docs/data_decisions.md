## 2. Invalid polygon: repaired with `buffer(0)`

1 outline(s) cross themselves. `buffer(0)` redraws the outline without the crossing; the repair is applied in the label loader, so every later step sees the same valid shape.

| tile | uid | part | damage | area before (px²) | area after (px²) | centroid moved (px) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| hurricane-michael_00000172 | 2aaab43d-9165-49eb-8dee-a16d86f40e57 | test | no-damage | 2582.73 | 2582.73 | 0.00 |

If `buffer(0)` ever splits an outline into several pieces, the loader keeps the largest piece, so a building is always one polygon. The audit scripts (`audit_data.py`, `dataset_stats.py`) load with `repair=False` so they keep reporting the outlines as annotated.

## 3. Tiny and edge-cut buildings: kept
