# Results (validation only)

Generated by `scripts/make_results.py` from `runs/`. **Every number here is on val-tiles or val. The test set (hurricane-michael, santa-rosa-wildfire) has not been loaded, evaluated or looked at**; `src/split.py` blocks it unless `--final` is passed.

- **val-tiles**: held-out spatial blocks of the six *training* disasters (14,718 buildings). Used for early stopping and for fitting the temperature, so scores on it are optimistic.
- **val**: hurricane-florence and midwest-flooding, never trained on (19,099 buildings). The honest estimate for a new disaster, but floods only, with few minor-damage and destroyed buildings (372 and 187), so its per-class numbers for those two classes are noisy.

Un-classified buildings are dropped (`docs/data_decisions.md`). Recall of a class = share of its buildings that were predicted as that class. Macro-F1 = plain average of the four per-class F1 scores.

## 1. Baselines

What you get without looking at any image. Class shares in train: no-damage 73.8%, minor-damage 8.8%, major-damage 9.4%, destroyed 8.0%.

| split | baseline | accuracy | macro-F1 | recall no-dmg | recall minor | recall major | recall destr. |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| val-tiles | majority class (no-damage) | 0.809 | 0.224 | 1.000 | 0.000 | 0.000 | 0.000 |
| val-tiles | random, weighted by train class frequency | 0.615 | 0.248 | 0.739 | 0.088 | 0.094 | 0.080 |
| val | majority class (no-damage) | 0.878 | 0.234 | 1.000 | 0.000 | 0.000 | 0.000 |
| val | random, weighted by train class frequency | 0.660 | 0.236 | 0.739 | 0.088 | 0.094 | 0.081 |

Majority-class accuracy is high because most buildings are undamaged; its macro-F1 shows it is useless. A model has to beat **both** numbers.

## 2. Sanity check: overfit 256 crops

Before any real training, the siamese model was trained on 256 training crops only (no augmentation) to check that the pipeline can learn at all: **100.0% accuracy on those crops after 4 epochs** (training loss 1.28 -> 0.026). So labels, gradients and learning rate are wired up correctly; it says nothing yet about generalisation.

- On **val-tiles**, `siamese` has accuracy 0.879, above the majority-class baseline (0.809), and macro-F1 0.708 against the baselines' 0.248.

The gap between val-tiles and val (macro-F1 +0.465) is the main result so far: the model has learned the training disasters, not damage in general. `docs/error_analysis.md` breaks this down per disaster.

## 4. Calibration (`siamese_s0`)

Temperature scaling (`src/calibrate.py`): one number T divides the logits. Fitted on val-tiles: **T = 1.598** (above 1: the model was over-confident and is made less sure). ECE = average gap between confidence and accuracy (15 bins); NLL = negative log-likelihood. Accuracy cannot change, because dividing by T never changes the top class.

| split | temperature scaling | ECE | NLL | mean confidence | accuracy |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| val-tiles | before | 0.0449 | 0.3492 | 0.9244 | 0.8795 |
| val-tiles | after | 0.0115 | 0.3127 | 0.8807 | 0.8795 |
| val | before | 0.2719 | 1.5206 | 0.7229 | 0.4510 |
| val | after | 0.1702 | 1.2401 | 0.6212 | 0.4510 |

![reliability diagrams](figures/reliability.png)

T was fitted on val-tiles, so the val-tiles 'after' number is the best case. The val rows show how well that one T carries over to unseen disasters.

## 5. Uncertainty methods (`siamese`)

Each method gives every prediction a confidence (`src/uncertainty.py`):

- **max prob**: the top softmax probability of the seed-0 model, raw and after temperature scaling;
- **MC dropout**: 20 passes with dropout left on; confidence = mean of the passes' top probabilities, or minus the entropy of the averaged probabilities *(not run yet)*;
- **ensemble**: probabilities averaged over models trained with different seeds *(not available: only siamese_s0 has been trained)*.

**Only the max-probability method is available in this version; MC dropout and the ensemble are waiting on the runs listed in `BLOCKERS.md`.**

ECE: lower = confidence closer to accuracy. AURC (area under the risk-coverage curve): lower = mistakes are ranked lower, and/or fewer mistakes. For the entropy row, ECE is computed on the top averaged probability, because minus-entropy is not a probability.

| split | method | accuracy | macro-F1 | ECE | AURC |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| val-tiles | max prob (raw) | 0.8795 | 0.7084 | 0.0449 | 0.0201 |
| val-tiles | max prob (temp. scaled) | 0.8795 | 0.7084 | 0.0115 | 0.0200 |
| val | max prob (raw) | 0.4510 | 0.2431 | 0.2719 | 0.3774 |
| val | max prob (temp. scaled) | 0.4510 | 0.2431 | 0.1702 | 0.3707 |
